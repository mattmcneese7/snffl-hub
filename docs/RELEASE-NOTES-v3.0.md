# Splash 3.0

The Squirtnite FFL app has a new name, a new look and a lot more under the hood. SQUIRT is now Splash. Squirt is still Squirt.

## A new look
- **Light and quiet.** Paper, white and powder blue, with deep blue for what matters and lime for the one thing you should notice. One typeface, Switzer, everywhere.
- **Widgets on a wallpaper.** Every page is a stack of cards over the league's own icon wallpaper, which drifts a little slower than the page as you scroll.
- **A new mark.** A deep blue drop with a football's laces and seams. It's the app icon, the header and the opening animation, and it's identical in all three.
- **One menu.** Home, The Rag, Scoreboard, The Feed, Standings, The Swami, More, each with the same icon as its widget on the Home.
- **One ticker.** Sleeper scores and NFL scores along the bottom, inside the same bounds as the menu.

## Home
- **The Drop.** On Sundays and Mondays a check-in leads the Home: games live, players left to play, how much of the week is played, your game with your chance to win, and the week's top score, Shart watch and closest game with Squirt's notes in the margin. Its icon pulses while games are live. Tap it to fold it away.
- **The Rag up top**, under its new red masthead.
- **Your game** opens your matchup in one tap, with the game's top scorer called out.
- **This week's chug** plays right on the Home. Every earlier chug lives in the archive on the Chug Meter page.
- **Hardware** sits right under the Scoreboard.

## Video that plays here
- **Highlights play inside the app**, muted until you tap, one at a time as you scroll. They come from NFL on FOX, CBS and NBC, CBS Sports, FOX Sports, Bleacher Report and more.
- **Phones get vertical clips first**, desktops get widescreen.
- **Plays only.** Press conferences and studio talk are filtered out, and no player gets more than two clips in a rail.

## Odds, your way
- **Pick your book** in Settings: DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, BetRivers or Polymarket. Every line in the app comes from your book and says so.
- **Compare the books** on every NFL game page, with the best number in each column marked.
- **Live odds.** Moneylines refresh every 20 minutes while games are on, and full lines twice a day and before every kickoff.
- **Sleeper props** on player pages: receptions, yards, touchdowns and more, with Sleeper's own lines and payouts.

## Deeper data
- **Insights**, a new page under More: draft steals and busts, usage risers and fallers by snap and target share, what every manager's pickups have scored, trending free agents nobody here has, and luck (record against all-play record).
- **Usage on every player page**: snaps, targets, target share, carries and air yards, week by week.
- **The Swami** keeps the trade evaluator and waiver suggestions, rebuilt to match, and folds away when you don't need it.

## Everywhere
- **Full names.** Players and NFL teams by their full names, stat lines spelled out ("6 catches on 11 targets"), and "Label | value" labels instead of jargon.
- **Player pages** rebuilt: who he is, who has him, and one button to propose a trade or claim him in Sleeper.
- **A trophy case**, a managers grid and a join page in the same style.

## One more thing
There's a way to visit every version of this app, SNFFL, Squirtnite and SQUIRT, live and working. It's hidden. Start at the bottom of More.
