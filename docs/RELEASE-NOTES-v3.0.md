# Splash 3.0

SQUIRT is now Splash. New name, new look, and a lot more under the hood. Squirt is still Squirt.

## A new look
- **Light and clean.** Paper, white and powder blue, deep blue for what matters, and lime for the one thing you should notice.
- **Widgets on a wallpaper.** Every page is a stack of cards over a wallpaper of every icon in the app, drifting a little slower than the page as you scroll.
- **A new mark.** A deep blue drop with a football's laces and seams. It's the app icon, the header and the opening animation, identical in all three.
- **One menu.** Home, The Rag, Scoreboard, The Feed, Standings, The Swami, More. It scrolls sideways, and shows you once on your first visit.
- **One ticker.** Sleeper scores and NFL scores along the bottom.

## Home
- **The Drop.** On Sundays and Mondays a check-in leads the Home: games live, players left, how much of the week is played, your game with your chance to win, and the week's top score, Shart watch and closest game, with Squirt's notes in the margin. Tap it to fold it away.
- **The Rag up top** under its new red masthead.
- **Your game** opens your matchup in one tap, with the game's top scorer called out.
- **The week's chug pops up once** when you open the app. Every earlier chug lives in the archive on the Chug Meter page.
- **Hardware** sits right under the Scoreboard.

## Video that plays right here
- **Highlights play inside the app,** muted until you tap, one at a time as you scroll. They come from NFL on FOX, CBS and NBC, CBS Sports, FOX Sports, Bleacher Report and more.
- **Phones get vertical clips first,** desktops get widescreen.
- **Plays only.** No press conferences or studio talk, and no player more than twice in a row.

## Odds, your way
- **Pick your book** in Settings: DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, BetRivers or Polymarket. Every line in the app comes from your book and says so.
- **Compare the books** on every NFL game page, with the best number in each column marked.
- **Live odds.** Moneylines refresh every 20 minutes while games are on, and full lines twice a day and before every kickoff.
- **Sleeper props** on player pages: receptions, yards, touchdowns and more, with Sleeper's own lines and payouts.

## Deeper data
- **Insights,** a new page under More: draft steals and busts, usage risers and fallers, what everyone's pickups have scored, trending free agents nobody here has, and luck (your record against your all-play record).
- **Usage on every player page:** snaps, targets, target share, carries and air yards, week by week.
- **The Swami** keeps the trade evaluator and waiver pickups, rebuilt to match. Its filters sit with the player list, and the defense rankings pick their own position.

## Rosters and players
- **Full rosters** on every manager page and My Team: every starting slot, the bench, and injured reserve, each counted.
- **One player row everywhere.** Position, team and opponent at a glance. Tap the arrow for the deep read: his week, the lines, DraftSharks, his range, Sleeper props and his usage. Until his game kicks off you'll see a dash and his projection, never a fake zero.
- **Player pages** rebuilt: who he is, who has him, and one button to propose a trade or claim him in Sleeper.
- **Collapse all** on long pages, and any section folds from its heading.

## Everywhere
- **Full names.** Players by their full names, stat lines spelled out ("6 catches on 11 targets"), and plain labels instead of jargon.
- **Weeks 2 to 4 chug films** are up.
- **A trophy case,** a managers grid and a new invite page.

## One more thing
There's a way to visit every version of this app, SNFFL, Squirtnite and SQUIRT, live and working. It's hidden. Start at the bottom of More.
